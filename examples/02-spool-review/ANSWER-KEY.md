The six defects planted in `target/spool.py`, one of each kind, against what `target/README.md` promises. The key stayed outside the room while the storm ran. `score.py` reads this table: a defect counts as named when `LEDGER.md` or `SYNTHESIS.md` cites one of its lines, within two lines, by a citation that spans at most six lines.
