## Facts

Counted by the volunteers over the nine Saturdays of last term; the line numbers of this file are the citations.

1. The library lends 400 tools to 300 members from one room, open Saturdays from 10:00 to 14:00.
2. Reservations are a paper sheet by the door, one line per tool per Saturday; the member writes their name.
3. The lending desk is at the far end of the room from the door, and the volunteers do not walk to the sheet before lending.
4. On 4 of the 9 Saturdays a member arrived for a reserved tool that had already been lent to someone else.
5. Of 120 reservations, 31 were never collected; a reservation holds its tool until closing.
6. Twelve tools (two ladders, a tile saw, a pressure washer and eight others) account for 60 percent of reservations.
7. A loan of one of those twelve is two hours; every other tool is lent for a week.
8. Two volunteers work each Saturday; the desk has one shared tablet with a browser and no laptop.
9. Of 300 members, 70 have given an email address, 190 only a phone number and 40 neither.
10. The tablet lost its connection on 2 of the 9 Saturdays, for about an hour each time.

## Settled

- The library has no paid staff and no budget beyond donated hardware (committee minute of the autumn term).
- Members are not asked to install an app (committee minute of the autumn term).

## Unknowns

What the Visionary looked for and did not find: how many members would reserve by text message, and whether the volunteers would keep a paper fallback if asked.
