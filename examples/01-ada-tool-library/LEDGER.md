## The graveyard

- **R1-A1-W · A Friday-night lottery for hot tools.** Here lies a lottery run on a Friday evening, killed by an evening that asks members and volunteers to be present together; the ground is silent on whether they would be. It may rise again with a volunteer who wants the job.
- **R1-A2-3 · The hold that outlasts the member.** Here lies a second statement of the release, killed by R1-A1-3, which says the same in fewer words and now stands as floor. Its objection, honest lateness, is carried into the synthesis as a risk.
