# Storm Ada — how should a tool library take reservations?

This storm is invented for the repository's example. Nothing in it is real: the library, its members, its numbers and its committee do not exist.

## The brief

> The Hollow Street Tool Library lends 400 tools to 300 members from one room, open on Saturdays. Reservations are a paper sheet by the door. On four Saturdays out of nine, someone arrives for a tool that was lent to another member while they held a reservation, and about one reservation in four is never collected. What should replace the paper sheet?

## The decision

What the committee adopts for the spring term, and what the volunteer who builds it is asked to build first.

## In scope · out of scope

In: how a member reserves, how a reservation is held and released, and what the two volunteers on a Saturday see. Out: the loan fee, the catalogue, and buying tools.

## Settled

The library has no paid staff and no budget beyond donated hardware (`GROUND.md:37`). Members are not asked to install an app (`GROUND.md:38`). Not re-opened.

## Criteria

1. A member never arrives to find a reserved tool already lent.
2. A volunteer runs a Saturday without learning anything new on the day.
3. A member with only a phone number is served as well as one with an email address.
4. It can be built and kept by one volunteer in their spare hours.

## The null

Keep the paper sheet as it is. It enters the tournament as the option "keep what we have".

## Assumptions

The facts of the ground are the committee's own counts of last term. The committee will try one change for four Saturdays before deciding on a second.

## Size

Spark. A narrow question, a two-way door (paper stays possible), and two analysts are enough for breadth.
