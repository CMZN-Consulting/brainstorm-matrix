# Grounds

The states the room has met, in which effort went to something other than the decision, or was lost. One line each, with what holds it now; a new entry also names the storm that met it. Analysts name a state under Grounds in their sheets. The Visionary files it here, and every synthesis adds what its storm met, or says none. The list is open; the routes are closed (the analyst skill, § Max effort and the thread).

- **G1 · The sheet grows with the effort, not with the question.** A sheet at max effort ran well past the size the same shape of question took at lower effort. Held by: 24 KB a sheet with its Coverage, and the artifact beside it.
- **G2 · The funnel keeps what it should kill.** Most of the cards stayed alive after the ledger. Held by: the ledger kills, at most four cards in ten alive.
- **G3 · The artifact lives in the sheet.** A draft artifact of over a hundred lines sat inside each sheet. Held by: the artifact in a file beside the sheet, and at most 20 lines of code in the sheet.
- **G4 · The card outgrows its shape.** Cards ran to twenty lines and more. Held by: 14 non-blank lines and 1.8 KB a card.
- **G5 · The synthesis carries everything.** One synthesis grew to several times a readable size. Held by: 10, 14 or 18 KB by size, and the TL;DR in eight lines.
- **G6 · The fourth sheet never seals.** A power cut left one analyst's sheet with nothing on disk. Held by: the sheet on disk before the first card, and a real box.
- **G7 · Doubts with no home.** Early sheets, written before the briefing rule, carried their doubts nowhere. Closed by `BRIEFING.md`: Doubts and Not read in every sheet.
- **G8 · The tree moves under the round.** Commits landed mid-round and analysts re-pinned their citations; one chased a shift of dozens of lines in a long file. Held by: the pinned commit.
- **G9 · Paragraph packing beats a line cap.** Cards of 14 lines ran to 5 KB, and helper bullets of 1.4 KB sat on one line. Held by: 1.8 KB a card.
- **G10 · Helper double work and the post-seal edit.** An analyst read its helpers' files itself while they ran, then edited the sheet after sealing; another pasted late helpers unread. Held by: the analyst skill's § Max effort and the thread, items 5 and 8: nothing edited after the seal, and the helper rules.
- **G11 · The unbriefed pilot overruns the box.** One pilot script grew into three and took two hours, and the next round was skipped. Held by: one falsifier script of two minutes, and a pilot only when the brief asks.
- **G12 · The brief and the skill disagree on tool use.** One analyst tried every combination of a tool and another refused every command. Held by: the brief's allowlist is the law for its round, and the skill says so.
- **G13 · The growth moves beside the sheet.** Under the caps the sheets held, and the files beside them grew instead: a sources table the size of a sheet, an artifact at half again its cap until a route split it. Held by: a cap on every artifact the brief names, and the peek's third signature applied to artifacts as well as sheets.
- **G14 · The allowlist is narrower than the skill.** A round's command allowlist omits steps the skill itself requires (a scratchpad, the clock, listing a directory, the helper audit), and the sheets disclose slips. Held by: an allowlist that names the skill's own steps.
- **G15 · The fetch tool is an agent.** When the analysts' sessions have no fetch tool, a web-fetch agent reads every page and a helper cap counts it. Held by: a fetch agent used as the fetch tool is a page read, uncapped; research helpers keep their cap.
- **G16 · The ruling moves under the storm.** The text under test keeps changing while the storm runs: new blocks land, a question is added mid-storm, and some of the room's points are ruled before the tournament. Held by: every round pins the text it reads; the tournament is re-pinned to the latest text, and the ledger lists what moved into the null, crediting the cards that met it; the text is checked once more right before the report.
